# Source conflicts and accounting distinctions

Current design source: [v1 group](../assets/sources/website-v1.json), `sr-whitepaper-v1`; prior [core](../assets/sources/website-core.json), [context](../assets/sources/website-context.json), [mint](../assets/sources/launch-mint.json) and [trading](../assets/sources/launch-trading.json) retain their own dates. Source publication is not implementation verification.

## Current contradictions: do not choose a formula

- **Launch taxes:** §12 calls the immutable launch opening **90% buy / 90% sell**, decaying to 2%/3%. §15 instead says **20% buy / 40% sell**, decaying linearly to the same 2%/3% steady rates over the launch window. The shared floors do not resolve the conflicting opening. `launch-trading-tax` and `launch-trading-tax-curve` remain unresolved, not silently assigned either schedule. The earlier launch announcement additionally described exponential decay; its body says a majority decays within the initial interval while its graphic calls that interval a half-life. Preserve current internal conflict and earlier source differences separately; no exact tax quote follows. [sr-whitepaper-v1: immutables, parameters; sr-post-2098969974044066298]
- **Daily auction decay:** §7 equation 7.1 interpolates geometrically from opening price to the floor over 24 hours; §§7–8 prose instead halves the gap to the floor every four hours and later settles at the floor. They are not generally the same price path, and the prose does not give the precise settlement cutoff. Both descriptions remain evidence, neither an endorsed executable curve. The now-published license floor does not resolve this discrepancy. [sr-whitepaper-v1: branches equation 7.1, auctions; auctions.md]

## Distinctions resolved or sharpened by current text

- **Paid versus historical free entry:** current §6 and the launch reply charge a whitelist liquidity fee. The earlier free founding-charter promise remains superseded history, not pricing. [sr-whitepaper-v1: charters; sr-post-2098969964283846751; sr-whitepaper-historical-free]
- **Founding versus ongoing receipts:** current §11 expressly gives the founding floor-bid/remainder allocation and excludes a team share, then separately gives the ongoing vault/POL/team split. This resolves the earlier blanket “all protocol ETH” wording tension; do not retain the split as unknown or route founding proceeds through the ongoing percentages. Founding proceeds escrow until finalization, unlike ongoing instant auctions' no-escrow description. [sr-whitepaper-v1: charters, reserves; sr-whitepaper: charters, reserves]
- **Ledger removals versus token burns:** current §3 distinguishes never-minted ledger value removed by licenses/fee burns from permanently burned tokens. Deposits destroy tokens only as a one-for-one ledger conversion and remain re-mintable. Both permanent categories lower the ceiling; deposit conversion is exempt. Do not equate ledger removal with circulating-token burn or assume expansion requires prior branch retirement. Exact callable paths/rounding still need implementation evidence. [sr-whitepaper-v1: currency]
- **Autonomy versus authority:** §12 states non-upgradeability alongside bounded owner tuning, an initially sole deployer owner without protocol-enforced delays, one-way switches and an optional narrowly powered guardian. A possible later Safe transfer is not an existing multisig configuration. Expansion purchases remain owner-only; withdrawal pauses are excluded from guardian power, but independent settlement failures remain possible. [sr-whitepaper-v1: exits, immutables]

## Remaining source and implementation boundaries

- **Issuance destination:** About says issuance goes into an LP; current whitepaper separates ledger accrual, withdrawal minting and genesis liquidity. The descriptions are not interchangeable. [sr-about; sr-app-about; sr-whitepaper-v1: currency]
- **Flow versus receipts:** the canonical pool is the source of the trading-flow signal, not the only source of protocol ETH; charter auctions also bring ETH. Current-sign fee routing and trailing-signal issuance decisions are different. Zero current flow routes to contraction; equation 5.2 does not equate a zero trailing signal with a negative signal. [sr-whitepaper-v1: entities, net-flow, policy]
- **LP fees versus protocol taxes:** the token page says protocol taxes apply on top of the LP fee. Token-side protocol-position fees are burned despite simplified ETH-flow diagrams. Exact fee bases, collection and ordering remain implementation questions. [sr-token-page-v1; sr-whitepaper-v1: currency, reserves, parameters]
- **Dormancy arithmetic:** bounty, revocation and stated remainder are visible, but bounty funding/deduction order is not. Transfers do not reset either wallet clock; the transferred charter has separate timestamp/grace treatment. Do not invent a net-payout formula or reduce activity rules to “any interaction.” [sr-whitepaper-v1: dormancy]
- **Launch timing and activation:** dated schedule uses EST in September without resolving daylight-saving interpretation. A relative “in 2 days” and explicit date are preserved with their displayed publication times. The current design ties liquidity/tax clock/emissions to finalization and makes license activation an owner action; neither earlier schedule nor current design proves execution. [sr-post-2098969960404103353; sr-post-2098969968201359736; sr-post-2098969982503915891; sr-whitepaper-v1: charters, branches, reserves]
- **Rendered data versus state:** baseline counters and newer displayed block/time anchors, not-launched pool and disabled-auction messages remain UI observations, not independently authenticated chain reads. The separately added deployment/condition records remain intact without proving ABI/source correspondence.
