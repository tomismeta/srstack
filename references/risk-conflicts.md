## Conflicts and distinctions that answers must preserve

- **Current versus historical entry cost:** current §6, mint page and launch thread charge the whitelist liquidity fee; older free-mint messaging is superseded, not current policy. See [updates](updates.md). [sr-whitepaper: charters; sr-mint; sr-post-2098969964283846751]
- **Genesis versus ongoing revenue:** §6 assigns genesis proceeds to liquidity/vaults without a team share, while §11 broadly says all protocol ETH follows the ongoing fee split. Keep the specific genesis exception explicit; do not silently route genesis receipts using the ongoing split. [sr-whitepaper: charters, reserves]
- **Issuance destination:** About opens with issuance into an LP; whitepaper §3 describes internal ledger accrual, withdrawal minting and a separate genesis LP pre-mint. The descriptions are not interchangeable. [sr-about; sr-app-about; sr-whitepaper: currency]
- **Autonomy versus controls:** immutable/no-board rhetoric coexists with an admin-set post-genesis charter reserve price, policy-controlled charter counts and a future transfer-enabling switch. Code immutability does not itself rule out configurable permissions; the controlling identities and boundaries are not established here. [sr-whitepaper: introduction, auctions, transfers]
- **Simplified versus detailed flows:** the net-flow section's trading-only entry/exit claim cannot describe all ETH receipts when charters are also sold for ETH. About's “fixed” issuance is pro rata within an epoch, not evidence of a permanently unchanging policy rate. About's “each branch” purchased at auction must be read alongside the included first branch. [sr-whitepaper: entities, net-flow, policy, branches; sr-about; sr-app-about]
- **Token fees versus ETH diagrams:** §11 explicitly burns fees earned in $STANDARD alongside its ETH-routing description; exact fee denomination/collection mechanics need contract evidence. [sr-whitepaper: reserves]
- **Ledger spending versus token burning:** sources describe spending earned balances on licenses while also saying issuance is initially an unminted ledger credit. They do not resolve whether licenses consume credits, wallet tokens or both, or how cancelled credits enter cumulative burns and issuance-budget accounting. Do not equate an advertised license burn with an observed reduction in circulating ERC-20 supply, or assume expansion requires a prior branch retirement. Redistribution has a similar credit-versus-token boundary. [sr-whitepaper: entities, currency, auctions, exits; sr-about; sr-app-about]
- **Dormancy arithmetic:** bounty, revocation fee and returned-remainder claims are visible, but their funding/deduction order is not specified. Do not invent a net-payout formula. [sr-whitepaper: dormancy]
- **Launch timing:** schedule uses EST in September, without resolving daylight-saving interpretation. Its graphic distinguishes token launch without liquidity from later seeded liquidity/emissions. The root's relative “in 2 days” wording and explicit event date must not be repaired by guessing publication time; consult the announcement record's displayed timestamp and retrieval limits. [sr-post-2098969960404103353; sr-post-2098969968201359736]
- **Tax body versus graphic:** body says a majority of the temporary tax decays within an interval; graphic calls that interval a half-life. Both are publisher statements, not the same exact mathematical rule or a verified tax quote. [sr-post-2098969974044066298]
- **Rendered presentation versus state:** zero counters, example charter diagrams and bank-health graphics do not establish zero supply, whitelist allocation, epoch state or holdings. The mint's not-live message is only a time-bounded website observation. [sr-protocol; sr-mint; sr-about; sr-app-about]
